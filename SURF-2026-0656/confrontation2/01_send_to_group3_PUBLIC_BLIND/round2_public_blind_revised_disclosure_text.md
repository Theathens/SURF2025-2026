# Round2 Real-Company ESG Disclosure Text

## Disclosure 001

In 2023, BYD carried out comprehensive waste heat recovery transformation for all painting workshops at its Pingshan core manufacturing campus. After recalculating production heat recycling efficiency, the revised Scope 1 direct fossil emission value dropped to 885320 tCO2e, representing a standard 5% downward adjustment. All carbon statistics are strictly limited to vehicle and battery production facilities; no full-group net-zero carbon neutral claims appear in any public disclosure materials.

## Disclosure 002

BYD finished full rooftop photovoltaic coverage across all Pingshan production plants in 2023, significantly lifting self-generated clean power proportion. Based on updated on-site power generation data, market-based Scope 2 emissions were revised down to 10839062 tCO2e with a 5% rational cut. This carbon dataset only covers manufacturing zones and excludes non-core group business segments.

## Disclosure 003

A brand-new blade battery mass production line was formally put into operation at BYD Pingshan base in 2023, driving moderate growth in overall factory power demand. The adjusted total electricity consumption reached 12047002 MWh after a standard 5% upward tweak, and we synchronized matching PV and green power growth data to keep energy logic consistent.

## Disclosure 004

Midea launched multiple overseas HVAC manufacturing workshops throughout 2024 to expand global product supply capacity. After matching actual production operation hours, total comprehensive energy consumption of all air-conditioning production bases was revised upward by 5% to 3704764 MWh, paired with expanded rooftop photovoltaic supporting facilities.

## Disclosure 005

In 2024, Midea replaced most gas-fired heating equipment with waste heat recovery devices in all domestic stamping workshops. Natural gas consumption was reasonably lowered by 5% to 65451176 cubic meters after recalculating stable production cycles, effectively cutting fossil energy input within manufacturing boundaries.

## Disclosure 006

Dual measures of rooftop PV expansion and workshop gas-saving renovation were fully implemented at Midea’s 2024 manufacturing parks. Combined Scope 1 plus Scope 2 carbon emissions of all HVAC production zones were adjusted down 5% to 2093300 tCO2e, with clear boundary limits on home appliance manufacturing only.

## Disclosure 007

All distributed photovoltaic arrays built on Shunde and Hefei Midea factory rooftops ran at full rated load in 2024. Annual self-generated photovoltaic power volume received an 8% reasonable upward revision to 336 GWh, which perfectly matches the rising total factory energy consumption level.

## Disclosure 008

Midea completed additional rooftop PV installation projects at its Chongqing home appliance production park during 2024. The overall installed photovoltaic capacity of all manufacturing bases was adjusted upward by 8% to 409 MW, corresponding to the newly added clean power generation equipment assets.

## Disclosure 009

Midea signed multi-year long-term green power procurement contracts in early 2024 to lock stable low-carbon electricity supply for all domestic production sites. The annual purchased green power volume was raised 8% to 54 GWh to align with expanded factory energy demand.

## Disclosure 013

Midea’s original 2024 public disclosure omitted two core indicators including air conditioner division electricity use and carbon credit retirement volume. We partially fill these two missing figures with an 8% adjustment range, and clearly restrict the data boundary to only core home air conditioner product lines.
