## Aurora Battery Systems Co. Ltd. 2023 Sustainability Report

### Company Overview

Aurora Battery Systems Co. Ltd. is headquartered in Suzhou, China, and manufactures battery cells, EV battery modules, stationary storage systems, and battery recycling services. In 2023, the reporting boundary covered the Linhai Battery Cell Base, the Chengdu Module Assembly Plant, and the Hefeng Recycling and Materials Center. Annual battery and storage-system output reached 22.5 GWh, with revenue of RMB 19800 million and 9800 employees.

### Reporting Boundary

The reporting boundary covered company-operated facilities and corporate operations related to the listed production sites. Scope 1 included natural gas combustion, diesel backup equipment, and site vehicles. Scope 2 used the market-based method and covered purchased electricity after market-based renewable electricity instruments. Scope 3 was prepared as a screening estimate covering purchased cathode materials, upstream transport, product distribution, and end-of-life screening estimate.

### Energy Use

Total energy consumption was 760000 MWh, including 671000 MWh of electricity, 72000 MWh of natural gas, and 17000 MWh of diesel. Direct energy consumption was 89000 MWh. Energy intensity was 33777.78 MWh per GWh of production.

### Renewable Electricity

Renewable electricity use reached 233000 MWh, representing 34.72% of total electricity consumption. This included 140000 MWh of purchased green electricity, 55000 MWh of green certificates, and 38000 MWh of self-generated rooftop photovoltaic electricity. The covered facilities were Linhai Battery Cell Base; Chengdu Module Assembly Plant; Hefeng Recycling and Materials Center.

### Greenhouse Gas Emissions

Scope 1 emissions were 20383 tCO2e and market-based Scope 2 emissions were 210240 tCO2e. Combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions were 230623 tCO2e. Scope 3 screening emissions were 1350000 tCO2e. Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions intensity was 10249.91 tCO2e per GWh.

### GHG Calculation Method and Data Quality

The company used simulated national grid residual factor; IPCC stationary fuel factors; supplier certificate allocation records and IPCC AR6 100-year GWP. Production coverage was 100.0% of the operating boundary. The annual data quality status was limited_assurance_energy_and_ghg_only; energy, greenhouse gas, and renewable-electricity records were reconciled against the annual metric panel and the green-power register.

### Energy Efficiency and Activity Data

The company recorded 9 energy-saving projects in 2023, with estimated energy savings of 19660 MWh and estimated greenhouse-gas reductions of 8454 tCO2e. Waste-heat recovery contributed 9200 MWh. These project-level activity data are disclosed separately from gross Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions.

Environmental performance was reviewed by the Board Sustainability and EHS Committee, which held 6 meetings during the year. Environmental and low-carbon training covered 9261 of 9800 eligible employees, equal to 94.5%. ISO 14001 coverage applied to 3 facilities.

### Carbon Credits and Claims

The company did not make a carbon-neutral claim for the reporting year and did not retire carbon credits for product-line or logistics claims.
